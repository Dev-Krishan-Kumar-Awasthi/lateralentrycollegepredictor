Detailed Registration Steps

1

Visit the MP Online DTE Portal

Open your web browser and navigate to the official DTE MP Online portal: dte.mponline.gov.in .

2

Select Course Type

Click on the "Select Course for Counselling" dropdown. Choose "Graduate Programs" and click on "B.E./B.Tech (Lateral Entry)".

3

Initiate Registration

Under B.Tech. Lateral Entry, select "Apply for Counselling". Click on "Registration". Read the declarations, check the consent box, and proceed to fill out the form.

4

Enter Personal Details

Fill in your name, parents' names, date of birth, and gender exactly as printed on your diploma marksheets. Provide your active email ID and mobile number to receive security alerts and OTP confirmations.

5

Diploma Academic Details

Select your diploma stream, branch specialization, passing status (completed/result awaited), and enter your cumulative CGPA or percentage marks. Verify these grades carefully to avoid mismatch rejection.

6

Upload Scanned Documents

Upload scanned copies of your diploma marksheets, photo, signature, domicile certificate (if residing in MP), and category/income certificates (within the specified KB limits). Use our Personalized Document Checklist to prepare.

7

Portal Payment & Locked Registration Slip

Submit the form, pay the online portal fee, and download/print your locked registration slip. **Save your temporary roll number and password safely** for the next step, which is choice filling.
