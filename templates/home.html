Common Questions — DTE MP Lateral Entry Rules 2025-26

Answers based on the official rules released by DTE Madhya Pradesh.

What is the minimum marks needed?

General (UR): At least 45% in your diploma.
SC / ST / OBC (MP): At least 40%.
Grace marks are allowed for eligibility, but they are not counted when calculating your percentage.

Who can apply for lateral entry?

Option 1: 3-year (or 2-year) Engineering Diploma from an AICTE/UGC recognized institute.
Option 2: B.Sc. degree (UGC recognized) with Mathematics in 10+2.
You must be an Indian citizen.

Is MP domicile compulsory?

Yes, for Govt. college seats — you need an MP Domicile certificate from Naib Tehsildar or Tehsildar.
For Private college UR seats, students from other states can apply — but only on seats that remain vacant after the last round.

What are the seat reservations?

From the MP quota seats:
• SC — 16%  |  ST — 20%  |  OBC (non-creamy layer) — 14%
• Female — 30% in each category (horizontal).
If female seats are empty, they go to Open (OP) of the same category first.

What is the 10% sports weightage?

If you have a Gold Medal from a National Level Sports Competition, you get 10% added to your qualifying exam marks.
You need a certificate from Director, Sports & Youth Welfare, Govt of MP (Proforma-9).
Note: these candidates are ranked after candidates with the same marks but no weightage.

What if two students have the same rank?

If two students have the same percentage:
1. The older student gets preference.
2. If age is also the same → names are compared in English alphabetical order.

Can SC/ST/OBC students get a general seat?

Yes. If an SC/ST/OBC student has a good enough rank to qualify in the UR (General) merit list, they are given a UR seat first. A reserved seat is only given if no UR seat is available in their preferred colleges.

How many lateral entry seats are there?

Maximum 10% extra seats on top of the sanctioned strength (supernumerary) from the previous year.
Also, any unfilled 1st year seats from the previous batch are added to this pool.

What documents do I need?

• Diploma / B.Sc. marksheets and certificate
• MP Domicile certificate (Proforma-6)
• Category certificate: SC/ST (Proforma-1), OBC (Proforma-2)
• TC + Migration (given to the college at admission)
• Sports certificate if applicable (Proforma-9)

Can I change colleges after admission?

No. Transfer to another college is not allowed once you take admission through lateral entry. This is a strict DTE MP rule.

Are TFW or EWS seats available?

No. TFW (Tuition Fee Waiver) and EWS quota seats are not available for lateral entry admissions as per DTE MP rules.

Is this tool official?

No. This is an independent tool built using public DTE MP data. It is not connected to DTE MP or any government body. Always verify on dte.mponline.gov.in.
