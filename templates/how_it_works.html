How MP DTE Counselling Works

Confused about the admission process? Don't worry. Here is a simple, step-by-step guide to help you get your B.Tech seat.

01

What is MP DTE?

MPDTE is the government department that manages admissions for Engineering (B.Tech), Pharmacy, and other technical courses in Madhya Pradesh.

Think of it like a single window — instead of going to every college, you apply here once, and they give you a seat based on your rank and preferences.
02

Choice Filling: The Most Important Part

After you register, you have to list the colleges and branches you like in order of priority. This is called your "Choice List". The order you choose is very important!

Example Choice List

4thY CollegeCSE
5thZ CollegeCSELast Resort
Important: Lock your choices carefully before the deadline. After locking, no changes allowed.
03

How do you get a seat?

DTE's computer checks your list one by one, starting from the top. For every choice, it checks:

Your merit rank meets the cutoff for that college + branch + category
A seat is still available in that quota

If both conditions are met → seat is allotted. If not → moves to your next choice. This repeats until a seat is found or all choices are exhausted.

04

The Counselling Rounds

Round 1

First seat allotment based on your merit rank and choice list. You may get a college from any position in your list.

Report to allotted college + pay fees + document verification.
Upgrade Round

Got a college in Round 1 but want a better one (higher priority choice)? Apply for upgrade. Your Round 1 seat is safe — you don't lose it.

Example: Got Z College (Choice 5) → Apply upgrade → If X College (Choice 3) has a seat → Upgraded! Otherwise, keep Z College.
Round 2

This is a fresh chance to get a seat. Cutoffs can go up or down depending on how many seats are left after Round 1.

Important: If you join Round 2, you might lose your seat from Round 1. Only do this if you are sure!
CLC (College Level Counselling)

Last option. If seats are still vacant after all DTE rounds, colleges conduct their own counselling. You go directly to the college and apply for a vacant seat.

No online process — visit the college physically with documents.
05

Complete Process Timeline

Registration & Document Upload

Register on dte.mponline.gov.in, upload marksheets, category certificate, domicile, etc.

Rank Published

DTE publishes merit ranks based on your CGPA/% in Diploma. Lower rank number = better merit.

Choice Filling & Locking

Fill and lock your priority list of college + branch combinations. Use our predictor to plan this!

Round 1 Allotment

Seat allotted based on rank + choices. Take admission or apply for upgrade.

Upgrade / Round 2

Try for a better seat. Weigh the risk vs reward carefully.

CLC (if still no seat)

Go directly to colleges with vacant seats for spot admission.

💡

Pro Tips for Choice Filling

Add as many choices as possible — more choices = more chances
Put your most preferred college + branch at the top
Include some "safe" choices (colleges where you are likely to qualify based on past cutoffs)
Use the Internal Branch Change (IBC) option after admission if you want to change branch within the same college
Check domicile requirements — Govt college seats mostly require MP domicile
