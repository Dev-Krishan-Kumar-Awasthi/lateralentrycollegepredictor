Counselling aur admission se jude sabse aam sawaalon ke jawab yahan payein.

Predictor Tools

Hamaara predictor 2024 aur 2025 ke Official DTE MP Cutoff Data par aadharit hai. Halanki ye 95%+ accurate trends dikhata hai, lekin har saal cutoff thoda upar-neeche ho sakta hai. Isliye ise ek guideline ki tarah use karein.

Multiple branches select karne se aapko un sabhi branches mein milne wale colleges ki list ek saath mil jati hai. Isse aapko counselling choice filling list banane mein asani hoti hai.

Counselling Rules

TFW seats par tuition fee maaf hoti hai. Ye seats sirf un students ko milti hain jinki family income 8 Lakh se kam ho (Income Certificate zaroori hai). Note: Lateral Entry mein TFW seats ka rule alag ho sakta hai, kripya official notification check karein.

Haan! MP DTE Lateral Entry ke rules ke mutabik, Diploma holders kisi bhi branch mein Engineering kar sakte hain. Example: Civil Diploma ke baad Computer Science (CSE) liya ja sakta hai.

General category ke students jinki family income kam hai, unhe 10% seat reservation milta hai. Iske liye "EWS Certificate" hona anivarya hai.

Admission Process

Round 1 main round hota hai. Round 2 un bachi hui seats ke liye hota hai jo Round 1 ke baad khaali reh jati hain. Round 2 mein aksar cutoffs thode high ho jate hain kyunki seats kam bachti hain.

Ye sabse aakhri round hota hai jo direct college mein jaakar hota hai. Ismein "First Come, First Served" ya Merit ke aadhar par spot admission milta hai.
