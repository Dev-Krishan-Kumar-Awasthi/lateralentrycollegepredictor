How is accuracy calculated?

What does accuracy mean here?
We predict your rank using 2024 data and compare it with 2025 data. The closer the percentile positions match, the higher the accuracy — showing our model is consistent and reliable.
What is the Opportunity Map?
Safe = 75%+ admission chance. Moderate = 40-74% chance. Reach = under 40% but still possible. Based on UR/OP category cutoffs from official DTE data.
Is this personalized?
Yes — all numbers are computed live from your CGPA. The opportunity map uses your estimated rank to calculate admission probability for every college-branch combination.
