1. Choice Filling Se Pehle Ki Taiyaari

📋 Sabse Pehla Step:

Choice filling karne se pehle apne college ki priority list (preference order) banakar rakh lo ek paper pe! Yahi sabse important step hai jo bahut log bhool jaate hain.

  • Socho ki tumhe konsa city pasand hai, konsi branch chahiye, aur GOVT vs Private mein kya preference hai — ye sab decide kar lo pehle.
  • Pichle saal ke cut-off zaroor dekh lo. Isse tumhe idea lag jayega ki tumhare marks se konse college mil sakte hain. Humari website pe College Predictor tool use karo iske liye!
  • Apna Roll Number, Date of Birth, aur Password ready rakhein — login ke liye ye sab chahiye hoga.

2. DTE Portal Par Login Kaise Karein?

  • 1 Sabse pehle dte.mponline.gov.in portal par jao.
  • 2 Apna Roll Number, Date of Birth, aur Password daalkar login karo.
  • 3 Login hone ke baad DTE portal tumhe colleges ki list dikhayega — ye list Alphabetical order (A to Z) mein hogi.
⚠️ Temporary Password Nahi Mila?

Agar tumhe temporary password nahi mila hai, toh portal par "Get Temporary Password" link par click karo. Wahan se tumhe naya temporary password mil jayega. Uske baad login karke apna password change karna mat bhoolna!

3. College Filter Karna (Chhantni)

DTE portal par bahut saare colleges hain. Tumhe unhe filter karke apni pasand ke colleges jaldi dhundne ka option milega. Filtering ke 4 tarike hain:

🏙️ Shehar (City)

Jis city mein padna chahte ho usse select karo — e.g. Indore, Bhopal, Jabalpur

🔧 Branch

Apni pasand ki branch choose karo — e.g. CSE, IT, Mechanical, Civil

🏛️ College Type

GOVT, Private, ya Self-Finance mein se choose karo

🔠 Alphabetical

A to Z order mein colleges dikhenge by default

4. College Ko Select Kaise Karein?

  • Tumhe jo bhi college chahiye, uske saamne wale checkbox (☐) ko tick (✓) karo. Jis order mein tick karoge, wahi tumhari preference order banega.
  • Colleges ko kramanusar (sequence mein) tick karna hoga — matlab pehle jo sabse zyada chahiye wo 1st choice, phir 2nd choice, aur aise hi aage.
  • Choice filling karte waqt pichle saal ke cut-off bhi dekh sakte ho portal pe. Isse tumhe idea lagta hai ki us college mein tumhare chances kitne hain.
💡 Smart Tip: Zyada se zyada colleges select karo! Kam choices = kam chances. Agar sirf 2-3 colleges daaloge aur wo nahi mile, toh koi seat nahi milegi. Minimum 10-15 choices zaroor bharo.

5. College Add, Remove aur Order Change Karna

Choice filling mein tumhe pura control milta hai — colleges ko add karo, hatao, ya order badlo. Ye sab payment se PEHLE hi karna padega:

College Jodna (Add):

Jis college ko choice list mein add karna hai, uske saamne wale checkbox (☐) ko check (✓) karo. Click karte hi wo tumhari list mein aa jayega.

College Hatana (Remove):

Agar kisi college ko list se hatana chahte ho, toh uske saamne ke checkbox ko uncheck kar do. Wo turant tumhari choice list se hat jayega.

Order Badalna (Reorder):

Colleges ka order change karne ke liye UP (↑) aur DOWN (↓) arrow buttons ka use karo. Jis college ko upar le jaana hai us par UP arrow press karo, neeche ke liye DOWN arrow.

Save Karna (Surakshit):

Payment se pehle, tum apni choice list ko baar baar change karke SAVE kar sakte ho. Jitni baar chahiye utni baar karo — payment karne tak tumhara order lock nahi hoga!

6. Fee Waiver Colleges Ke Saath Normal Choices Bhi Bharo!

🎓 DTE Ka Official Advice:

DTE keh raha hai ki candidates ko Fee Waiver wale options ke saath-saath saamanya (normal) options bhi zaroor bharein.

Kya matlab hai? Kuch colleges mein SC/ST/OBC students ko fees mein discount ya full waiver milta hai. Lekin un seats ki sankhya limited hoti hai. Agar tum sirf fee waiver wale colleges bharte ho aur wo nahi milte, toh tumhe koi bhi seat nahi milegi.

Isliye fee waiver + normal dono tarah ke colleges bharo taaki admission ka chance double ho jaye!

7. Payment Ke Rules — Bahut Important! 🚨

SABSE ZAROORI RULE — DHYAN SE PADHO!

Payment karne se pehle apni college choice list ka order 100% confirm kar lo ki sahi hai.

⚠️ Payment karne ke BAAD college ke order mein kisi bhi tarah ka koi change NAHI hoga!

Matlab agar tumne galti se kisi kam pasand wale college ko pehle rakh diya aur payment kar diya, toh ab wo FINAL hai. DTE isko change nahi karega.

  • Payment Karne Ke Baad: Apna status check karne ke liye "Check Candidate Status" link par jaake apna payment confirm zaroor kar lo. Agar status pending ya failed dikhta hai, toh turant DTE helpline ko contact karo.
  • Payment Modes: Payment online hi hota hai — Net Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card, ya UPI se. Payment successful hone ke baad receipt download aur save kar ke rakhna.

8. Password Ko Surakshit Rakhein 🔒

  • Temporary password mila hai toh pehli baar login karke turant apna password change karo aur naya strong password set karo.
  • Changed password ko kisi notebook ya phone mein likhkar safe rakhein. Agar password bhool gaye toh choice filling nahi ho payegi jab tak reset nahi hota.
  • Kisi ke saath apna password share mat karo. Koi aur tumhare account se login karke tumhari choice list badal sakta hai — jiski zimmedari sirf tumhari hogi.

9. Pro Tips & Choice Filling Strategy 🧠

🎯
Top 3-5 Dream Colleges Pehle Rakho:

Sabse pehle apne dream colleges (jinmein sabse zyada admission chahiye) daal do. Chance kam ho toh bhi — loss kuch nahi hai, fayda ho sakta hai!

Realistic Choices Beech Mein Rakho:

5th se 10th choice tak wo colleges dalo jinmein tumhare marks ke hisaab se moderate chances hain. Ye tumhara safe zone hai.

🛡️
Safety Colleges End Mein Rakho:

Last 5-7 choices mein wo colleges dalo jahan tumhara admission almost pakka hai (based on past cut-offs). Ye tumhari safety net hai.
