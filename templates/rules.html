DTE MP Counselling 2026-27

Full Rules Simple Hinglish Explanation

Ye document DTE MP Counselling Portal ka official counselling guide hai. Main ab tumhe ek bhi important point miss kiye bina simple Hinglish me samjha raha hu.

1. Counselling Kisliye Hai?

Ye counselling MP ke:

  • Government colleges
  • Autonomous colleges
  • Private colleges
  • University colleges

me admission ke liye hoti hai.

Courses: Diploma, B.Tech, BCA, BBA, MBA, MCA, Pharmacy, Architecture, M.Tech etc.

2. Counselling Ka Full Flow

  1. Registration
  2. Document Verification
  3. Choice Filling
  4. Choice Lock / Submit
  5. Merit List
  6. Seat Allotment
  7. Upgradation
  8. College Reporting & Admission
  9. Internal Sliding
  10. CLC Round
  11. Admission Cancellation

3. Registration Rules (Sabse Important)

Rule 1 — Registration Sirf Ek Baar

Ek candidate ko counselling me sirf 1 baar registration karna hota hai. Baad ke rounds me dubara registration nahi karna.

Rule 2 — Registration Free Hai

Normally registration free hai. Lekin: NRI, IPS, PIO/FN/CIWGC, CLC specific cases me fees lag sakti hai.

Rule 3 — Roll Number Bahut Important

Registration: JEE Main Roll No ya Qualifying exam roll no se hota hai.

System automatically Name, Father name, DOB fetch kar lega. Agar galat info bhari → admission cancel ho sakta hai.

Rule 4 — E-Verification

System automatically verify karega: MP domicile, SC/ST/OBC certificate, Board marks.

Agar sab verify ho gaya: → “Registration cum e-verification slip” milegi.

Rule 5 — Agar Verification Nahi Hua

Agar auto verification nahi hua to documents manually upload karne honge.

Rule 6 — Documents Clear Hone Chahiye

Blur document upload kiya: → directly NOT ELIGIBLE ho sakte ho. Responsibility tumhari hogi.

Rule 7 — Grade System Wale Students

Agar marks ki jagah CGPA ya Grade diya hai, to conversion formula bhi upload karna compulsory hai. Aur marksheet + formula merge karke single JPG banana hai.

Rule 8 — Multiple Marksheet Wale

Agar multiple marksheets hain to university ka final passing certificate upload karna hoga.

Rule 9 — Password

Registration ke baad mobile par temporary password aayega. Usko change karo aur kisi ko mat batao.

4. Cancel Registration Rule

Bahut important. Agar registration me galti ho gayi to:

  • “Cancel Registration” option use kar sakte ho.
  • Same roll number se dubara register kar sakte ho.

Phir documents dubara verify honge.

5. Edit Registration Rule

Last Date Ke Baad

Verified candidates ko sirf 1 baar Edit Registration ka chance milega. BUT choice lock hone ke baad edit nahi hoga.

Edit Karne Ke Liye

Help center jana padega.

Edit Karke Submit Karna Mandatory

Agar edit karke submit nahi kiya: → current round se bahar ho jaoge.

6. Document Verification Rules

Verification Ke Baad

Candidate Eligible ya Not Eligible declare hoga. SMS bhi aayega.

Agar Verification Se Khush Nahi

To cancel registration, correct documents upload, aur re-verify kar sakte ho.

Final Verification College Me Hoga

Online verification ke baad bhi college reporting ke time original documents check honge. Agar mismatch mila: → admission instantly cancel.

7. Choice Filling Rules (Bahut Important)

Eligible Candidate Hi Choice Bhar Sakta Hai

Verification ke baad hi choice filling allowed hai.

Maximum Choices Bharna Better Hai

DTE ne officially advise kiya: jitni zyada choices bharoge utna better.

TFW Seats Rule

Agar TFW eligible ho to sirf TFW mat bharna. Saath me normal seats bhi bharna. Kyunki reserved category students ko scholarship already mil sakti hai.

Submit Aur Fees

Sirf choice save karna enough nahi hai. Final steps (Submit aur Fees payment) mandatory hai. Fees nahi di: → choice lock nahi hogi.

8. Choice Editing Rule

Choice filling edit karne ka extra chance mil sakta hai. Fees: ₹100 portal fee (Non-refundable).

9. Partial Tuition Fee

Counselling me jo ₹1000 partial tuition fee doge, wo college fee me adjust ho jayegi. Agar admission nahi liya: → amount confiscate ho jayega.

10. BPL & Class-IV Employees Rule

MP BPL aur Class-IV employee children ko special benefit milta hai. But cutoff merit ke andar hona zaruri hai. Applicable in Diploma, B.Tech, BCA, BBA etc.

11. Merit Rules

Merit JEE rank ya qualifying exam marks par banegi.

12. Allotment Rules

Seat allotment merit, reservation, aur choices ke basis par hota hai.

13. Upgradation Rules (B.Tech Important)

Agar first round me seat mil gayi to upgrade option choose kar sakte ho.

Upgrade Ka Meaning

Tumhari allotted branch se better preference wali branch mil sakti hai.

Upgrade Fees

Extra fee: partial tuition fee aur portal fee dena hota hai.

Upgrade Hone Par

Old seat automatically cancel ho jayegi. New allotment letter download karna padega.

Upgrade Nahi Hua

To old seat valid rahegi.

First Choice Mila To Upgrade Nahi

Agar pehli choice hi mil gayi: → upgrade option nahi milega.

14. Admission Rules

Original Documents Le Jana

College reporting ke time original documents mandatory hain.

Final Admission Kab Complete Mana Jayega?

Jab candidate ke password se admission lock hoga, tab admission final.

Admission Slip

Admission slip print, sign, aur safely keep karna mandatory hai.

TC & Migration

College sirf TC aur Migration original rakhega. Baaki original documents nahi dene.

Extra Fees Illegal Hai

College AFRC approved fee ke alawa extra paisa nahi le sakta. Official fee website: AFRC MP Official Website

15. Internal Branch Sliding

Sirf B.Tech/B.E. me hota hai. Counselling ke baad college ke andar branch change chance milta hai. Fees: ₹100. Agar branch change ho gayi: automatic update ho jayega.

16. CLC Round (College Level Counselling)

Bahut important round hota hai.

CLC Kya Hai?

Jab centralized counselling ke baad seats bachti hain, to colleges khud admission dete hain.

CLC Merit Kaise Banti Hai?

Jo candidate 10:30 AM – 1 PM tak physically present hoga, uski merit banegi.

CLC Registration Mandatory

Sabko registration compulsory hai.

CLC Me Real-Time Photo

Live photo capture mandatory hai.

CLC Provisional Admission Risk

Agar documents verify nahi hue to provisional admission mil sakta hai. Later verification fail hua: → admission cancel.

17. Admission Cancellation Rules

Admission Cancel Karwana Hai To College ya help center jana hoga.

Important Lock Period

Choice lock hone ke baad aur allotment se pehle admission cancel option available nahi hota.

Fraud/False Info

Agar fake certificate, wrong info, ya hidden facts mile: → kabhi bhi admission cancel ho sakta hai.

Fee Refund Rule

Academic calendar ke according certain last date ke baad fee refund nahi milega.

18. Special Seat Types

Available seats: TFW, EWS, BPL, NRI, IPS, AI seats, JKM, JKR, PIO/FN/CIWGC

19. Reservation Rules

MP Reservation: SC → 16%, ST → 20%, OBC → 14% (OBC creamy layer excluded)

20. Horizontal Reservation

Extra reservation: Ex-servicemen, Freedom fighter, Divyang, Technical stream

21. Important Fees

  • Normal Courses: First time ₹1530 total (Includes counselling fee, partial tuition fee, portal fee)
  • Diploma: ₹330
  • Upgrade: ₹1030
  • Edit Registration: ₹30

22. B.Tech Eligibility

Required Subjects: 12th me Physics, Maths hone chahiye.
Minimum Marks: General: 45%, SC/ST/OBC: 40%
Admission Basis: Mainly JEE Main rank

23. Diploma Eligibility

10th pass enough.

24. MCA Eligibility

Any graduation + Maths required.

25. MBA Eligibility

Graduation: 50% (reserved → 45%). Admission: CMAT basis.

26. Important Warnings

  • Original Documents Mat Jama Karna: Except TC, Migration.
  • Agents Se Bachna: Kisi दलाल, agent ko documents ya money mat dena.
  • SMS & Email Save Karna: Har allotment, verification, reporting message save rakhna.

27. Most Important Practical Advice

Ye Galti Mat Karna:

  • ❌ Sirf 5–10 choices bharna → risky
  • ❌ Sirf top colleges bharna → seat miss ho sakti hai
  • ❌ TFW only bharna → dangerous
  • ❌ Blur documents → rejection
  • ❌ Fees payment pending → choice lock nahi hogi
  • ❌ Password share karna → account risk
