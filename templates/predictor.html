Am I Eligible? — Check Eligibility Before Predicting
Route 1 — Diploma Holders
  • 3-year or 2-year Engineering Diploma in ANY branch
  • From AICTE/UGC recognized institute
  • Min 45% marks (UR)  |  Min 40% (SC/ST/OBC-MP)
  • Grace marks holders eligible — but grace marks excluded from % calc
Route 2 — B.Sc. Graduates
  • B.Sc. Degree from UGC recognized university
  • 10+2 with Mathematics as a subject required
  • Min 45% marks (UR)  |  Min 40% (SC/ST/OBC-MP)
Domicile & Seat Info
  • MP Domicile certificate mandatory for Govt. seats
  • Certificate issued by Naib Tehsildar / Tehsildar
  • Lateral entry seats = 10% supernumerary (over sanctioned intake)
  • Out-of-state candidates: only on vacant seats in last round
Seat Reservation (MP Quota)
  • SC — 16% of MP Quota
  • ST — 20% of MP Quota
  • OBC (excl. creamy layer) — 14%
  • Female — 30% horizontal in EACH category
Reserved category candidates: If your merit qualifies for UR (General) category, you will be allotted a UR seat first. Reserved seat is allotted only if no UR seat is available as per your preference.  |  Transfer: Once admitted, transfer to another institution is NOT permitted.  |  TFW/EWS: Not available for lateral entry as per DTE MP rules.
