Why we built this tool?

Choosing the right college after Diploma can be stressful. We built this tool to help students in Madhya Pradesh find the best colleges based on their marks, without having to search through confusing official documents.

How it helps you

Instead of guessing, you can use our College Predictor and Rank Predictor to see where you stand. It's fast, accurate, and completely free.

The idea is simple: You give us your CGPA, and we show you the colleges that usually accept students with your profile. No more manual searching through hundreds of pages of PDF cutoffs.

Data Source

The prediction system is built using publicly available counselling data released by the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Madhya Pradesh. Data covers the 2024 and 2025 lateral entry counselling rounds.

Download official PDFs and notices from the DTE MP counselling portal. Our database is built from the same public cutoff records.

Official DTE MP Portal Counselling Documents

How does it work?

1. Turning CGPA into Rank
We check your CGPA against past DTE merit lists to see what your rank might be. This is based on real data from official merit PDFs.
2. Finding Your Match
We look for colleges where the closing rank in previous years was similar to yours, keeping your category and branch in mind.
3. Comparing 2024 & 2025
We show you results from both years so you can see if a college is getting harder or easier to get into.
Is it 100% Guaranteed?
No tool can guarantee a seat because cutoffs change every year. But this gives you the best possible estimate to plan your choice filling.

Documents Required for Counselling

As per DTE MP Lateral Entry Rules 2025-26, the following documents are required:

Academic Documents
  • ✓ Diploma / B.Sc. final marksheet & certificate
  • ✓ All semester marksheets
  • ✓ 10+2 marksheet (for B.Sc. route — must show Mathematics)
  • ✓ Grade-to-marks conversion certificate (if grading system)
Identity & Domicile
  • ✓ MP Domicile certificate — Proforma-6 (from Naib Tehsildar/Tehsildar)
  • ✓ Photo ID proof
  • ✓ TC (Transfer Certificate) — to be deposited in admitted institution
  • ✓ Migration certificate — to be deposited in admitted institution
Category Certificates
  • ✓ SC/ST certificate — Proforma-1 (from competent authority)
  • ✓ OBC certificate — Proforma-2 (excl. creamy layer)
  • ✓ OBC Income certificate / Self-declaration (Proforma-10) — if OBC cert before 30 Apr 2022
  • ✓ Sports Gold Medal certificate — Proforma-9 (if claiming 10% weightage)
Original documents are returned after verification during counselling. Only TC and Migration are to be deposited in the admitted institution. Do not submit original documents to any unauthorized person or institution.
